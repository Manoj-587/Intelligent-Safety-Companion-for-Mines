# Underground Mine Fire — Emergency Response

## Immediate Actions (First 60 Seconds)
1. **Raise the alarm** — Activate the nearest fire alarm or call the surface control room immediately.
2. **Assess the fire** — Only attempt to fight the fire if it is small, you have a clear escape route, and you have been trained to use the extinguisher.
3. **Do not use water on electrical fires** — Use CO2 or dry powder extinguisher only.
4. **If the fire cannot be controlled immediately** — Do not waste time. Evacuate.

## Evacuation Procedure
1. Warn all personnel in the affected area.
2. Move upwind of the fire — smoke and toxic gases travel with the ventilation airflow.
3. Use the designated escape route. Do not use the intake airway if it is smoke-filled.
4. Activate your self-rescuer if smoke is present.
5. Proceed to the nearest refuge chamber or surface.
6. Report to the assembly point and confirm your presence to the supervisor.
7. Do not re-enter the mine until the all-clear is given by the mine manager.

## Conveyor Belt Fires
Conveyor belt fires are the most common underground fire type.
- Stop the conveyor immediately.
- Isolate the electrical supply to the conveyor.
- Use CO2 extinguishers on the belt.
- Activate the conveyor fire suppression system if installed.

## Communication
- Inform the surface control room of: location, nature of fire, number of personnel in the area.
- Keep communication lines open throughout the emergency.

## After the Emergency
- Do not re-enter until ventilation has cleared all smoke and gases.
- A competent person must inspect the area and confirm it is safe.
- Complete an incident report.
