# Methane Gas Emergency Response

## When the Gas Monitor Alarms
1. **Stop all work immediately.** Do not operate any electrical switches.
2. **Do not create any ignition source** — no sparks, no open flames, no mobile phones.
3. **Increase ventilation** — if it is safe to do so, increase airflow to dilute the gas.
4. **Alert the supervisor and surface control room** immediately.
5. **Withdraw personnel** from the affected section if concentration exceeds 1.0% CH4.

## Methane Outburst (Sudden Large Release)
A methane outburst is a sudden, violent release of gas from the coal seam.
1. Evacuate the section immediately — do not wait for instructions.
2. Activate your self-rescuer.
3. Move to the return airway only if it is not gas-filled.
4. Proceed to the nearest refuge chamber or surface.
5. Do not re-enter under any circumstances until declared safe.

## Ignition Has Occurred
If methane has ignited:
1. Activate the fire alarm.
2. Evacuate all personnel immediately using the designated escape route.
3. Isolate all electrical equipment in the affected area.
4. Inform the surface control room of the location and extent of the ignition.
5. Do not attempt to fight a methane fire — evacuate and let the mine rescue team respond.

## After the Emergency
- The area must be inspected by a competent person with calibrated gas detection equipment.
- Ventilation must be restored and confirmed adequate before re-entry.
- All personnel must be accounted for at the surface assembly point.
- An incident report must be completed.

## Prevention
- Maintain gas monitoring equipment in calibrated condition.
- Never bypass or disable gas interlocks.
- Report any increase in gas readings to the supervisor immediately.
