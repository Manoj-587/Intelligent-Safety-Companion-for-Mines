# Mine Collapse and Roof Fall — Emergency Response

## Immediate Actions
1. **Move away from the fall area immediately** — secondary falls are common.
2. **Raise the alarm** — contact the surface control room and supervisor.
3. **Account for all personnel** — determine if anyone is trapped.
4. **Do not attempt uncontrolled rescue** — further disturbance can cause secondary collapse.

## If Personnel Are Trapped
1. Establish communication with trapped personnel if possible (voice, tapping signals).
2. Inform the surface control room of: location, number of trapped persons, condition.
3. Keep trapped personnel calm — instruct them to conserve air and energy.
4. Do not attempt to remove large debris without engineering assessment.
5. Wait for the mine rescue team.

## Warning Signs Before a Collapse
- Cracking or popping sounds from the roof or ribs.
- Visible cracks or spalling in the roof strata.
- Roof supports showing signs of excessive load (bending, cracking).
- Increased water seepage from the roof.
- Vibration sensor readings above normal thresholds.

## Immediate Evacuation Triggers
Evacuate the section immediately if:
- Roof supports are visibly failing.
- Continuous cracking sounds are heard.
- Vibration sensors (MM252, MM261, MM262, MM264) are reading above safe thresholds.
- Water inrush is occurring.

## After the Emergency
- The area must be barricaded and declared unsafe until inspected by a mining engineer.
- Ground support must be reinstated before re-entry is permitted.
- All personnel must be accounted for.
- An incident report must be completed.
