# Bearing Inspection and Maintenance

## Why Bearings Fail in Mining Equipment
Mining equipment operates in harsh conditions — dust, moisture, vibration, and heavy loads accelerate bearing wear. Early detection prevents catastrophic failure and unplanned downtime.

## Warning Signs of Bearing Failure
- **Abnormal vibration** — detected by sensors MM261, MM262, MM263
- **Elevated temperature** — detected by infrared sensors AMP2_IR, DMP3_IR
- **Unusual noise** — grinding, squealing, or rumbling sounds
- **Increased current draw** — motor working harder due to bearing drag
- **Visible grease leakage** — seal failure allowing contamination

## Inspection Steps
1. **Visual inspection**: Check for grease leakage, discolouration (heat), and physical damage.
2. **Temperature check**: Use an infrared thermometer. Normal operating temperature: 60–80°C. Above 100°C requires immediate investigation.
3. **Vibration check**: Compare current vibration readings against baseline. A 25% increase warrants investigation.
4. **Noise check**: Use a stethoscope or vibration pen to listen to the bearing while running.
5. **Lubrication check**: Verify correct grease type and quantity. Over-greasing is as harmful as under-greasing.

## Maintenance Actions by Severity
| Condition | Action |
|---|---|
| Slightly elevated vibration | Increase monitoring frequency, check lubrication |
| Temperature 80–100°C | Reduce load, check lubrication, schedule replacement |
| Temperature above 100°C | Shut down equipment, replace bearing before restart |
| Grinding noise | Shut down immediately, replace bearing |

## When Sensors MM261 or MM262 are Elevated
1. Identify which equipment the sensor is monitoring.
2. Perform a physical inspection of bearings on that equipment.
3. Check lubrication levels and condition.
4. If vibration is confirmed abnormal, reduce load and schedule maintenance.
5. Do not continue operating equipment with confirmed bearing failure.

## Lubrication Guidelines
- Use the grease type specified in the equipment manual.
- Re-grease at the intervals specified by the manufacturer.
- Purge old grease when re-greasing to prevent over-packing.
- Keep grease nipples clean before and after greasing.
