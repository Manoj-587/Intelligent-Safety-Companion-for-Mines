# Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) — Underground Mining

## Mandatory PPE for All Underground Personnel
Every person entering an underground mine must wear:
1. **Hard hat** — protects against falling objects and roof falls.
2. **Cap lamp** — primary light source; must be charged and functional before entry.
3. **Self-rescuer** — worn on the belt at all times; provides 60 minutes of breathable air in a gas emergency.
4. **Safety boots** — steel-toed and ankle-supporting; protects against falling objects and ankle injuries.
5. **High-visibility vest** — ensures visibility in low-light conditions.
6. **Reflective clothing** — required in all areas where mobile equipment operates.

## Task-Specific PPE
| Task | Additional PPE Required |
|---|---|
| Blasting | Blast shield, hearing protection, dust respirator |
| Drilling | Hearing protection (>85 dB), dust respirator (P2 minimum), eye protection |
| Welding / cutting | Welding shield, fire-resistant gloves, fire-resistant clothing |
| Electrical work | Insulated gloves (rated for voltage), arc flash protection |
| Handling chemicals | Chemical-resistant gloves, face shield, chemical apron |
| Working near conveyors | Tight-fitting clothing (no loose ends), hearing protection |
| Roof bolting | Hard hat with chin strap, eye protection, dust respirator |

## Gas Hazard PPE
- In areas with known gas hazards, carry a personal gas detector at all times.
- Self-rescuer must be immediately accessible — not stored in a bag.
- In areas with H2S risk, use a supplied-air breathing apparatus (SABA), not a filter respirator.

## PPE Inspection
- Inspect all PPE before each shift.
- Do not use damaged or expired PPE.
- Report damaged PPE to the supervisor immediately.
- Self-rescuers must be inspected and certified at the required intervals.

## Important Reminders
- PPE is the last line of defence — engineering controls and safe procedures come first.
- Wearing PPE incorrectly provides no protection. Ensure correct fit.
- Never remove PPE in a hazardous area, even briefly.
