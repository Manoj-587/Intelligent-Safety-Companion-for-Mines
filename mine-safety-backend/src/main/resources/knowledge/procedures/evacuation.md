# Emergency Evacuation Procedure

## When to Evacuate
Evacuate immediately when:
- The mine alarm sounds.
- You detect smoke, fire, or strong gas smell.
- Your gas detector alarms.
- You hear continuous cracking sounds from the roof.
- You receive an instruction to evacuate from any supervisor or safety officer.
- Visibility drops due to smoke or dust.

## Evacuation Steps
1. **Stop work immediately.** Switch off equipment if it is safe to do so in under 5 seconds. Do not delay evacuation to shut down equipment.
2. **Warn others.** Alert all personnel in your immediate area.
3. **Activate your self-rescuer** if smoke or gas is present. Do not wait until you feel symptoms.
4. **Use the designated escape route.** Know your primary and secondary escape routes before each shift.
5. **Move upwind** — away from smoke and gas, towards fresh air.
6. **Do not use the cage or skip** unless instructed by the winding operator. Use the man-riding conveyor or travel road.
7. **Proceed to the surface assembly point.** Do not stop at intermediate levels.
8. **Report to the supervisor** at the assembly point. Confirm your name and that you are safe.

## If the Escape Route is Blocked
1. Do not panic. Activate your self-rescuer.
2. Proceed to the nearest refuge chamber.
3. Seal the door and activate the communication system inside the chamber.
4. Wait for rescue. Conserve air and energy.
5. Signal your location by tapping on pipes or structure at regular intervals.

## Assembly Point Protocol
- All personnel must report to the designated surface assembly point.
- The supervisor must account for every person on the shift register.
- Do not leave the assembly point until released by the mine manager.
- Inform the supervisor immediately if any person is unaccounted for.

## Self-Rescuer Use
1. Remove from belt holster.
2. Pull the activation ring.
3. Insert the mouthpiece and bite firmly.
4. Apply the nose clip.
5. Breathe normally through the mouth only.
6. The device provides 60 minutes of protection — use this time to reach the surface or a refuge chamber.
